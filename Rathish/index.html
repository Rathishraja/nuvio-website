ABOUT
Builder. Evaluator. Speaker. Mentor.

I started out making things because I was curious. That curiosity took me from art and design into technology, entrepreneurship and eventually into rooms where I now get to evaluate, mentor and speak to the people building what comes next.

As the founder of IOXIT Tech and a product designer, I spend a lot of time turning ideas into something tangible — products, experiences, brands and solutions. I care less about how impressive an idea sounds and more about whether it actually works.

That same mindset followed me to the other side of the table. I've evaluated teams at Smart India Hackathon in 2024 and 2025, served on a national-level Statathon panel, mentored student teams and been invited to speak and conduct sessions at colleges.

I've been the student with an idea. I've been the person building it. And now, I'm often the person sitting across the table asking, “Does this actually solve the problem?”
